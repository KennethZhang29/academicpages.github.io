--- title: "Energy-Constrained Drone Delivery: State-Aware Guided Search" excerpt: "A proposed extension from static edge guidance to move- and route-state-aware optimization for payload-dependent delivery with optional charging stations." collection: portfolio ---

This research direction extends learning-guided routing into energy-constrained drone delivery. The setting couples route order, payload-dependent energy consumption, charging decisions, and feasibility repair.

The methodological focus is a hybrid model: edge-level priors retain global route structure, while move- and state-aware signals guide local repair and adaptation. The planned evaluation covers scalability, robustness under distribution shift, and ablations that separate the contribution of each signal.

The broader goal is to build optimization systems whose learned components improve practical decision quality while remaining interpretable and anchored in reliable search.
